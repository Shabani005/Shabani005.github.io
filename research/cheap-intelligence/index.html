Cheap intelligence: routine decisions without long reasoning traces

Routine decisions (expenses, SLA breaches, tickets, ledgers) have short answers. Answer them with reasoning off, let a small decision model score each answer, and escalate only the doubtful ones: 2.5× fewer output tokens and 47% lower cost on 300 back-office tasks.

Limitation

The model was never trained to work with a gate: rejected answers mostly come back unchanged, so gating every step of a long task falls apart.

Next step

Post-train the model on this run's graded records so it defers and revises by itself, then re-run the same tasks.
